Adaptive learning powered by AI: Braivo turns existing educational content into a personal AI tutor.

For educators, schools, training providers, and teams building learning products from content they already own.

- **Bring your own content.** A syllabus, slides, PDFs, a question bank — Braivo works from the material you already have.
- **Adapt to each learner.** Each learner spends time on what they need to learn or remember next, rather than moving through the same fixed sequence as everyone else.
- **Keep your source content authoritative.** AI structures, enriches, and adapts your material; it does not silently replace the source with unrelated generated material.
- **Ship it as your product.** Run Braivo under your own brand and domain, or integrate it through its HTTP API. No Braivo branding required.
- **Works with your stack.** Braivo is an adaptive-learning layer rather than another LMS, designed to sit alongside the systems you already use.

What a learner sees next comes from an explicit, testable learning model rather than an LLM prompt alone, so each decision can be explained from the evidence behind it.

The core platform — learning model, content processing, and APIs — is developed in the open at [braivo/braivo](https://github.com/braivo/braivo). Braivo's own code is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only, with commercial licensing available for organizations that need different terms.

**Status:** pre-release and under active development. Learn more or request a demo at [braivo.app](https://braivo.app), or write to <hello@braivo.app>.
